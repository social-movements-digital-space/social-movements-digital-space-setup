1 What Zotero Is Used For

Zotero is a reference manager.

We will use it to help organize academic literature used during the seminar.

Zotero can store:

  • journal articles;
  • books;
  • book chapters;
  • reports;
  • webpages;
  • PDFs;
  • citation information.

You do not need previous experience with Zotero.

2 Create a Zotero Account

Go to:

Zotero

If you do not already have an account:

  1. choose Register;
  2. create a free account;
  3. confirm your email address;
  4. sign in.

Use an email address you can continue to access during the semester.

3 Install Zotero

Download Zotero from:

Download Zotero

Choose the version for your operating system.

Install Zotero using the normal/default installation options.

After installation, open Zotero.

You should see the Zotero library interface.

4 Sign In to Zotero

Inside Zotero:

  1. open Settings or Preferences;
  2. locate the Sync settings;
  3. enter your Zotero account information.

This connects the Zotero application on your computer with your Zotero account.

5 Install the Zotero Connector

The Zotero Connector allows your browser to send references directly to Zotero.

Go to:

Zotero Connector

Install the connector for your browser.

After installation, you should see a Zotero button or icon in your browser toolbar.

Depending on the webpage, the icon may look like:

  • a document;
  • a journal article;
  • a book;
  • a folder.

6 Test the Zotero Connector

Open an academic journal article or another publication in your browser.

Click the Zotero Connector icon.

Choose your Zotero library when asked where to save the reference.

Then open Zotero.

The reference should appear in your library.

7 Check the Metadata

Click the saved reference in Zotero.

Check whether the basic information looks sensible:

  • author;
  • title;
  • year;
  • publication;
  • DOI or URL.

Automatic metadata is useful, but it is not always perfect.

You should get used to checking important references before using them in academic work.

8 Course Group Library

As a first exercise and to familiarize yourself with the content: Create your own library for the course and load the course literature into Zotero.

Note

Joining library groups does not replace your own personal Zotero library.

You can continue using your personal library independently.

Start a good habit today and continuously build your own library thought your studies.

9 Save One Test Reference

Before considering the setup complete:

  1. open one academic article in your browser;
  2. save it using the Zotero Connector;
  3. open Zotero;
  4. confirm that the reference appears;
  5. inspect the title, author, and publication information.

10 Optional: PDFs

Depending on the source and your access permissions, Zotero may also save a PDF together with the citation information.

Do not worry if every reference does not automatically include a PDF.

Access to full-text documents depends on the publication and your institutional access.

11 Common Problems

11.1 The browser button is missing

Check that:

  • the Zotero Connector is installed;
  • the extension is enabled in your browser;
  • the browser has been restarted if necessary.

11.2 Zotero is installed but references do not appear

Check that:

  • Zotero is open;
  • the Zotero Connector is enabled;
  • you selected the correct Zotero library.

11.3 I cannot see the course group

Check that:

  • you accepted the Zotero group invitation;
  • you are signed into the correct Zotero account;
  • Zotero synchronization is enabled.

11.4 The saved metadata is wrong

Automatic metadata extraction is not always perfect.

You can edit metadata directly in Zotero.

Check important references before using them in assignments.
